System designing

Thermodynamic HVAC-R Layout Design

Every premium building setup requires specialized thermal planning. AERC provides custom system design services for MEP contractors and consultants.

  • Heat load calculation utilizing standard psychrometric parameters.
  • Custom open-type and L-type condenser calculations (with options for Blue or Gold fins).
  • Piping layouts for VRF/VRV refrigerant lines, preventing pressure drops.
Cold Chain Setup

Turnkey Cold Storage & Blast Freezing

AERC is a leading channel partner facilitating food processing and cold chain development. We customize layouts for dairies, agricultural storages, and pharmaceutical hubs.

  • Modular cold storage planning with varying PUF panel thickness (60mm to 150mm).
  • Air-cooled and water-cooled scroll/reciprocating condensing units from Copeland.
  • Evaporator coil sizing (Blue Cold, Guntner) for optimal relative humidity.
Technical Sizing

Equipment Sizing & Sourcing Support

AERC is a leading distribution stockist. We collaborate with architects, HVAC consultants, and MEP contractors to help select the exact specifications of climate and refrigeration equipment for large-scale integration:

  • Heat load calculation & model selection advising for VRF layouts.
  • VRF/VRV copper piping diameter selection with Rajco metal specifications.
  • Sizing guidance for panel thickness and PUF density selection in cold chain layouts.
Customer Care

Large Ready Stock & Prompt Technical Support

Critical cooling systems require immediate spare support. Because we maintain the largest ready inventory of Copeland compressors, SRF gases, and Danfoss valves in Central Maharashtra, we can provide immediate replacements, minimizing downtime.

  • In-house support desk to address technical queries.
