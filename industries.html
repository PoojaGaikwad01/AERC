Industries We Serve

Specialized Climate Control & Cold Chain Execution Across Sectors

  • Home
  • Industries

Residential Projects

We supply premium high-efficiency split AC systems and multi-zone VRV/VRF setups for modern luxury residences, apartments, and villas, ensuring personalized room comfort and low power bills.

Corporate & Commercial Buildings

For commercial workspaces and corporate buildings, we plan central air conditioning solutions using cassette ACs, ducted splits, and energy-saving VRF layout designs with smart centralized thermostats.

Hotels & Restaurants

Hospitality requires dynamic thermal zones. We supply guest room cooling, kitchen ventilation setups, back bar refrigerators, under-counter coolers, and pastry displays from Western Refrigeration.

Hospitals & Pharmaceuticals

Sterile air is critical. We provide specialized clean room HVAC engineering, operation theatre laminar flow designs, and pharmaceutical storage medical freezers from Blue Star and Godrej.

Cold Storages & Warehouses

We build modular cold rooms, walk-in coolers, and large-capacity blast freezers. Backed by Emerson Copeland condensing units to store fresh agri-products, seeds, and frozen foods.

Food Processing & Dairy Industry

Specialized cooling cycles for pasteurization, dairy processing, beverage chilling, and confectionery manufacturing. We design brazed plate heat exchangers and shell-and-tube evaporators.

Manufacturing Industries

Industrial process chilling, control panel cooling systems, heavy-duty electrical panel ACs, and workshop comfort air systems designed for high-ambient operations.

Retail Stores & Supermarkets

We supply attractive Visi-coolers, island freezers, upright chillers, and glass-door displays that maintain precise cold temperatures while maximizing item visibility for retail customers.

Educational Institutions

We supply central cooling systems for university libraries, auditoriums, science laboratories, and high-capacity stainless steel drinking water coolers from Usha and Blue Star.
