Since 1991

A Pioneer in HVAC-R Product Distribution

Ajanta Electric & Refrigeration Co. was established in 1991 in Nirala Bazar, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra. It operates as a leading local trader, supplier, and service provider specializing in cooling and refrigeration appliances.

AERC operates as a premium corporate supplier and distribution hub. Recognizing the industry's need for authentic, high-quality HVAC components and specialized technical support, we have built direct factory channel partnerships with global leaders like Copeland, Danfoss, Daikin, Cruise, Voltas, and Carrier.

Our core specialty lies in our strong technical background. Rather than simple supply, we consult with architects, mechanical consultants, and MEP contractors to specify the correct scroll compressors, select optimized evaporators, configure condensing units, and plan sizing for large cold storages and commercial layouts.
