Corporate Overview

Wholesale HVAC-R Distribution Since 1991

Ajanta Electric & Refrigeration Co. (AERC) has been a trusted pioneer in the Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration (HVAC-R) supply chain for over three decades.

Based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), we specialize in wholesale trading, technical equipment selection, and distribution logistics. AERC serves as an authorized wholesaler, SSD distributor, and channel partner for leading global cooling brands. We maintain Aurangabad's largest ready stock of OEM compressors, copper pipelines, and gases, backed by dedicated technical support.
